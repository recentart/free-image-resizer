Privacy

Last updated 3 October 2026.

Your images

Images you open are resized by your own browser and never leave your device. They are not uploaded to our server or to anyone else, and we can't see them. The page's security policy blocks it from sending data anywhere.

No accounts, no analytics

There's no sign-up and no analytics or tracking code of our own. The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which processes standard request data (such as IP addresses) to deliver and protect the site.

Advertising

The site can show a few small banner ads from Adsterra, an advertising network. Each ad runs in its own sealed-off (sandboxed) frame, so ad code can't see your images or the rest of the page.

Adsterra and the advertisers it works with may use cookies and collect technical data, such as your IP address, browser and device type, to show and measure ads. Adsterra explains how it handles this data in its privacy policy. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time.
