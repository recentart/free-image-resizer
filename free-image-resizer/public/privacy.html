Privacy

Last updated 2 October 2026.

Your images

Images you open are resized by your own browser and never leave your device. They are not uploaded to our server or to anyone else, and we can't see them. The page's security policy blocks it from sending anything to our server.

No accounts, no analytics

There's no sign-up and no analytics or tracking code of our own. The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which processes standard request data (such as IP addresses) to deliver and protect the site.

Advertising

The site shows a few small ads served by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners show you ads based on those visits.

You can turn off personalised advertising in Google's Ads Settings, or opt out of some other vendors' cookies at aboutads.info. Google explains how it uses data from sites that show its ads in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. Where the law requires it, you'll be asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.
